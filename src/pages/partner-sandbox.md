# Partner Sandbox

A partner sandbox is a Marketo Engage instance that partners use during the software development lifecycle. Partners can use a sandbox to explore the Marketo Engage UI and access Marketo Engage API services.

Adobe provisions a sandbox after a partner is accepted into the [Adobe Exchange Partner Program, Digital Experience](https://partners.adobe.com/technologyprogram/experiencecloud.html).

In most respects, a partner sandbox is functionally equivalent to a production instance. However, partner sandboxes do not include add-on modules and have fixed capacity limits.

## Capabilities Summary

- Sandboxes: One per partner
- API calls: 50,000 per day
- Lead database records: 10,000
- Email sends: 30 per campaign run
- Custom objects: Two
- Users: 25
